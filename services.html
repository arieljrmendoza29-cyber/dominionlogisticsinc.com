Core Services

Seven integrated systems. One operational plane.

Purpose-built software and infrastructure for logistics operators — from dispatch and routing to settlement and modernization advisory.

Service 01

Dispatch & Fleet Routing Engines

Automated route optimization algorithms, dynamic real-time asset tracking, load sequencing, and centralized dispatch management — the operational core that keeps fleets moving without friction.

  • Route optimization tuned for time windows, driver hours, and vehicle constraints
  • Real-time asset tracking with continuous geofence and status updates
  • Intelligent load sequencing across multi-stop runs
  • Centralized dispatch board with drag-and-drop assignment
Service 02

Operational Infrastructure & TMS Integration

Architecture engineered to unify legacy Transportation Management Systems, custom APIs, and operational data pipelines into a single high-throughput platform — without ripping out what already works.

  • Bi-directional TMS integrations with major and legacy platforms
  • Custom API layers for internal tools, partners, and shippers
  • Event-driven data pipelines for real-time operational state
  • Cloud-native infrastructure sized to fleet volume
Service 03

Driver Workflow & Mobile Applications

Intuitive mobile tools designed for the cab — automated turn-by-turn routing, digital proof of delivery (ePOD), duty status tracking, and instant dispatch updates that keep drivers focused on the road.

  • Turn-by-turn routing tuned for commercial vehicles
  • Digital proof of delivery with signature and photo capture
  • Hours of Service and duty status tracking
  • Two-way messaging and real-time dispatch push updates
Service 04

Predictive Maintenance & Fleet Health Systems

Sensor and telematics data integration designed to monitor vehicle health, anticipate maintenance needs, and reduce unplanned fleet downtime — turning reactive repair into scheduled reliability.

  • Telematics ingestion from major ELD and sensor providers
  • Predictive alerts for component wear and failure risk
  • Maintenance scheduling tied to duty cycles and mileage
  • Fleet-wide health scoring and cost-per-mile visibility
Service 05

Data Analytics & Operations Dashboards

Custom business intelligence suites offering granular visibility into fuel efficiency, lane profitability, driver performance metrics, and operational throughput — decision-ready, not decoration.

  • Lane-level P&L and profitability analysis
  • Fuel efficiency and idle-time benchmarks
  • Driver performance scoring and safety metrics
  • Throughput, on-time delivery, and SLA dashboards
Service 06

Automated Billing & Freight Settlement Systems

Integrated modules for digitizing rate sheets, streamlining carrier payments, generating automated invoices, and auditing freight bills — end-to-end financial infrastructure for freight ops.

  • Digitized rate confirmation and contract management
  • Automated invoice generation and delivery
  • Carrier payment workflows and settlement cycles
  • Freight bill auditing with exception routing
Service 07

Logistics Systems Consulting & Modernization

Strategic technical advisory to evaluate legacy software stacks, eliminate operational bottlenecks, and architect custom cloud infrastructures for growing operators — long before the code gets written.

  • Legacy stack audits and modernization roadmaps
  • Cloud architecture design and migration planning
  • Vendor consolidation and integration strategy
  • Operational bottleneck diagnostics and remediation
