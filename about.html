About Us

The logistics layer, re-engineered.

Established in October 2022, Dominion Logistics, Inc. delivers enterprise-grade software and systems solutions tailored specifically for logistics and transportation companies.

Who we are

Logistics expertise meets systems architecture.

Operating at the intersection of logistics expertise and systems architecture, our mission is to eliminate operational bottlenecks through technology.

We develop intelligent dispatch engines, dynamic routing platforms, and comprehensive management dashboards that give operators total oversight of their assets and data.

Rather than moving physical cargo, we move the intelligence behind it — equipping transportation businesses with the tools needed to scale efficiently in an increasingly complex global market.

How we operate

Principles that shape every build.

  1. 01

    Ship what operators actually use

    We design with dispatchers, drivers, and fleet managers in the room. Every workflow answers a real question someone asked in a real terminal — not a boardroom.

  2. 02

    Integrate, don't isolate

    Legacy TMS platforms, ELDs, telematics feeds, and accounting stacks stay. We unify them into one high-throughput operational plane — without forcing a rip-and-replace.

  3. 03

    Data is only useful when it's actionable

    Dashboards should drive decisions, not decorate meetings. Every metric we surface is tied to something a person can change — lane pricing, driver assignment, maintenance windows.

  4. 04

    Reliability is a product feature

    Freight doesn't wait. Our platforms are engineered for uptime, redundancy, and predictable performance under load — because a stalled dispatch queue is a stalled fleet.
